# User-selected everyday models

The six independent examples illustrate selected features, rather than every event in a phase. Worksheets illustrate copying information; sleeping bags illustrate compact packing without changing the contents; paired gym students illustrate alignment; socks illustrate equal distribution; garden fences illustrate two enclosures; a soap-bubble model illustrates a pinching shape. The biology descriptions and source references still come from the supplied textbook. The bubble caption explicitly distinguishes a modelled shape from living cells dividing their cytoplasm and organelles.

Every scene retains four different kinds or pairs where it represents DNA information. The original/copy sheets share identical markings; the two baskets and gardens contain matching sets. No additional biological mechanisms are introduced by the analogy labels.
